The craft

From fire to gold.

I

Gather

Molten glass is gathered from the furnace at around 1,100 °C. The heavy base is shaped while it still glows.

II

Form

The wall is blown and turned to an even thickness, so the glass feels balanced in the hand and rings clear when it's touched.

III

Gild

The rim is banded in gold, gold leaf is set into the base, and the medallion is applied by hand.

IV

Finish

Every piece is inspected, polished and packed with its walnut coaster.
